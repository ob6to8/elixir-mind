# Contract-size counterweight

The [epistemology review](/meta/analysis/epistemology-and-governance-review.md)
§5: the contract loads into every session (15,171 words at 2026-08-01); the
five longest policies (skills-registry 1,209 · session-capture 1,146 ·
banned-phrases 1,136 · controlled-type-vocabulary 1,110 ·
elixir-coding-standards 791 words) carry roughly a third; the terseness rule
exists but nothing measures it, and every mechanism ratchets obligations in —
the banned-phrases register is monotonic by construction. **Re-measure at
delivery**: this matter runs last in its sequence precisely so the baseline
is the post-sweep text.

**Deliver two halves — separable at approval:**

1. **The warn-only trend instrument.** Per-policy and total word counts,
   trend not gate, per the
   [coding-standards](/meta/policy/elixir-coding-standards.md) admission rule
   (offline, dependency-free — a small `mix` task or a fold into an existing
   report; a checker earns a *gate* only on the standing admission rule, and
   this one deliberately never gates).
2. **The relocation disposition.** The review's candidates: the
   skills-registry behavioral specs and the session-capture rationale
   paragraphs, relocated to the existing tutorial layer with the policy
   keeping the terse rule text and a link. Decide direction and executor —
   the [complete-docs-rewrite plan](/meta/plans/complete-docs-rewrite.md)'s
   open question 1 asks whether policy prose is in its scope, so the
   disposition here either assigns relocation to that plan or scopes it as
   its own follow-on matter. One artifact per matter; do not run the
   relocation inside this delivery unless the operator directs it.

Edge: the
[compile-skills-registry-from-skill-frontmatter plan](/meta/plans/compile-skills-registry-from-skill-frontmatter.md)
(operator-designated priority 1) would shrink or retire the largest policy —
factor its status into the relocation disposition at delivery.
