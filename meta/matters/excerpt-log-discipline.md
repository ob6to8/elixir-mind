# Excerpt-log discipline

The [content review](/meta/analysis/content-quality-sample-review.md)'s
erosion 1, re-measured 2026-08-02 post-fence-fix: 112 of 164 knowledge docs
carry a `## Thread excerpts — route-tagged log` section — 4,037 of 20,492
lines, **19.7% of the knowledge corpus**, growing in absolute terms with
every capture. In sampled docs the log exceeds the authored body; multi-ref
regions lift identical ~60-line blocks verbatim into every named sink; one
tagged paste re-embedded an entire raw Reddit thread inside a distilled
reference. The machinery is contract-sanctioned and freshness-gated — the
weak joints are **selection** (what gets tagged) and **whole-region lifting
with no size discipline**.

**Deliver two halves in one ruling:**

**1. The selection rule** — amend
[route-tagging](/meta/policy/route-tagging.md) (a new settled-property bullet
beside "Per-paragraph, multi-ref, lifted whole"), `/render-contract` riding.
Drafted in the originating thread, for ratification verbatim or amended:

> **Select the feeding paragraph, not the surrounding run.** A tag region
> covers the paragraph(s) that feed the sink's matter — the narrowest span
> that carries what the sink accretes — never the wider exchange for
> convenience. A paragraph that merely contains a wholesale external paste
> is not itself the feeding content: the paste files as its own capture
> document, and the tag covers the discussion of it.

**2. The mechanics ruling** — decide the direction on each lever (execution
may emit a follow-on plan per
[governance-artifact-routing](/meta/policy/governance-artifact-routing.md) if
the chosen mechanics warrant design):

- **Per-sink size cap** in the materializer — warn vs. fail, and the
  overflow form (link to the thread region instead of lifting it).
- **Render-collapsed** on the Pages site — presentation-only; does not
  reduce what agents load and grep, so it complements rather than replaces
  the cap. (Edge: the
  [response-resource-links / Pages-sunset matter](/meta/matters/response-resource-links-pages-sunset-revision.md)
  may moot the site half — check its state at delivery.)
- **The retro question** — whether the existing 112 sinks' tags are
  re-selected under the new rule (4,037 lines are already lifted; the
  selection rule alone only slows the ratio), and if so whether as one
  sweep or opportunistically on touch.

Recommendation carried from the originating thread: ratify the selection
rule as drafted; cap + collapse designed together with the retro pass
decided there (the interactions with the verifier's divergence gate and
re-materialization need design, not a bare ruling).
