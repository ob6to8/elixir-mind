# Comprehension precedes acceptance

This is a **standing direction**: agent-produced work enters the brain — merges,
lands, becomes precedent — only after the operator comprehends it at the
altitude that matters. Comprehension is a *precondition of acceptance*, not a
debt to be recovered later by the record.

## The direction

The brain's first era ran acceptance ahead of comprehension, deliberately: the
operator vetted intent at a high level, skipped diffs without a specific
concern, deferred implementation minutiae, and accepted the resulting exposure
as the price of speed — betting that "well enough" would not compound into
unrecoverability while a mental model of the system accumulated. That bet's
known cost is **slop**, in the operator's definition:

> "unvetted architecture that optimizes for the local considerations of the
> agent at the time of authoring vs the high level intent and direction of the
> repo." — [journal, 2026-07-28](/journal/2026-07-28.md)

This doctrine closes that era. The standing direction is the inverse ordering:
**nothing is accepted on the trust that its author's local optimization matches
the repo's intent; acceptance follows the operator's own comprehension.** Speed
is spent to buy comprehension, not the reverse.

## The altitude — comprehension is layered, not uniform

Comprehension-before-acceptance does not mean reading every line. The required
altitude follows the artifact's layer:

- **Code** is comprehended at the contract level — "on the level of functions,
  types, and call stacks" ([journal, 2026-07-28](/journal/2026-07-28.md)) —
  with implementations left opaque exactly where
  [opacity has been earned by the oracle](/meta/doctrine/intent-is-the-source.md),
  and descended into wherever it has not.
- **Governance and prose** (policies, doctrine, plans, skills) are comprehended
  in full — they bind agent behavior directly, so there is no lower altitude at
  which accepting them is safe.
- **Where comprehension fails, acceptance waits.** A thing the operator cannot
  yet state the role and interface of is a question to resolve, never a detail
  to wave through.

## What it commits the brain to

- **The acceptance gate is the operator's comprehension, not the agent's
  self-verification.** An agent's assurance that its work is correct — however
  detailed — does not substitute for the operator holding the model. The agent's
  duty under this direction is to *present at the comprehension altitude*:
  surface the decisions, interfaces, and consequences plainly rather than
  burying them in implementation detail.
- **Deferral is scheduling, not acceptance.** Punting an unwieldy matter into a
  plan parks the *work*; it must not silently park the *comprehension* — the
  plan is read and understood before it is ratified, like any governance text.
- **Comprehension produced is comprehension filed.** Understanding gained while
  vetting is baked into the knowledge base — tutorials, glossary terms, moduledoc
  and code-map upgrades, issues for what is found wrong — per
  [governance-artifact-routing](/meta/policy/governance-artifact-routing.md),
  so it compounds instead of evaporating with the session.
- **The backlog is a named debt.** Work accepted under the prior ordering is
  presumed under-comprehended until audited; the
  [comprehension audit plan](/meta/plans/comprehension-audit.md) is that debt's
  remediation. The audit ends; this direction does not.

## Relationship to sibling doctrines

- [The operator must be able to reconstruct a mental model of code they did not write](/meta/doctrine/comprehension-of-generated-code.md)
  mandates the *capability*: the brain's design must make comprehension
  producible. This doctrine mandates the *occurrence*: that comprehension
  actually happens, and happens **before** acceptance. Capability without
  occurrence was precisely the first era's posture.
- [Intent is the source; opacity is earned by the oracle](/meta/doctrine/intent-is-the-source.md)
  bounds this direction from below: earned opacity defines the floor beneath
  which comprehension is *not* required. This doctrine adds that everything
  above that floor is comprehended ex ante, not on demand.
- [The engineer as orchestrator](/meta/doctrine/engineer-as-orchestrator.md)
  charges the operator with quality evaluation; this doctrine fixes *when* that
  evaluation is due — at the gate, not downstream of it.
