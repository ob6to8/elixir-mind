# isnād

The chain itself is graded, not just recorded: each transmitter's reliability
is assessed ([rijāl](/beliefs/glossary/rijal.md)), a chain is only as strong as its
weakest link, and gaps in the chain (a hop nobody can account for) demote it.
Independent chains carrying the same statement can corroborate each other, but
a sound chain does not by itself validate the statement's content — see
[matn criticism](/beliefs/glossary/matn-criticism.md). The ISNAD framework
(arXiv:2607.24117) transfers this machinery to multi-agent AI pipelines,
treating each hop a claim takes — scraper, extractor, model, synthesizer — as
a transmitter in its own graded chain.
