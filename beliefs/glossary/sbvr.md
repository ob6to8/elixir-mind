# SBVR (Semantics of Business Vocabulary and Business Rules)

Its two modalities are the reason it comes up in governance design:
**alethic** statements assert what must be true by the structure of the domain
(a rule that cannot be broken because breaking it makes the model incoherent),
while **deontic** statements assert what an actor is obliged to do (a rule that
*can* be broken, with consequences). That maps onto the distinction between a
[policy](/beliefs/glossary/policy-type.md) — an enforceable obligation — and a
[doctrine](/beliefs/glossary/doctrine.md) — a standing direction that
shapes judgment. It carries no modality for belief, which is the gap
[doxastic logic](/beliefs/glossary/doxastic-logic.md) fills.

Being freely downloadable, it is also the practical substitute for a paid
terminology standard when the need is definition craft in a controlled
vocabulary rather than the authoritative text of
[ISO 704](/beliefs/glossary/iso-704.md).
