# belief (type)

The controlled `type` sitting parallel to [doctrine](/beliefs/glossary/doctrine.md), not beneath it: a belief is epistemic-with-values ("I hold that the world works this way"), a doctrine is teleological (the brain's own standing direction). It never carries `verified` — a belief that turns out to be empirically checkable is refiled as a `claim`, and the type boundary is the test. Defined in the [controlled-type vocabulary](/meta/policy/controlled-type-vocabulary.md); the functional role it gives a home to is the [decision prior](/beliefs/glossary/decision-prior.md).

A survey of four candidate standards — [ISO 704](/beliefs/glossary/iso-704.md), [SKOS](/beliefs/glossary/skos.md), [PROV-DM](/beliefs/glossary/prov-dm.md), and [SBVR](/beliefs/glossary/sbvr.md) — found none carrying a modality for belief, so the axis is this bundle's own. Its nearest formal anchor is [doxastic logic](/beliefs/glossary/doxastic-logic.md), which yields the filing test in one line: if removing *I hold that…* changes what warrants the sentence, it is a belief rather than a claim.
