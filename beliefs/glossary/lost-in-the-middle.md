# lost in the middle

A positional failure mode, distinct from (but compounding) [context rot](/beliefs/glossary/context-rot.md)'s occupancy-driven degradation: instructions stated once at the top of a long thread effectively go stale as the window grows. It is the failure [recitation](/beliefs/glossary/recitation.md) counters — rewriting the objective into the recent end of the context instead of trusting the middle to hold.
