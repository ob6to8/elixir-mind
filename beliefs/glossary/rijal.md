# rijāl

Rijāl criticism grades a transmitter, not a single transmission — a narrator's
reputation for honesty and accuracy accumulates across the many statements
they are known to have relayed over a lifetime, which is what lets a later
scholar weigh a hearsay report at all. The ISNAD framework's transfer of this
to multi-agent pipelines keeps a graded reliability score per (narrator,
domain) pair, but grades a model call rather than a person; the
[captured discussion thread](/knowledge/SWE/agentic/provenance/isnad-reddit-discussion-thread.md)'s
sharpest critique is that a model call has no persistent identity the way a
human narrator does — its reliability is a property of the model plus the
task type plus what was in context, so grading at the model level alone
produces a number that "is stable and says nothing."
