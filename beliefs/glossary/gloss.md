# gloss

From Greek *glōssa* (tongue, language), by way of the marginal and interlinear
annotations medieval scribes wrote beside difficult words. A
[glossary](/beliefs/glossary.md) is a *collection* of glosses, so the two share a
root — but "gloss" is not an abbreviation of it, and the distinction is worth
holding because this bundle uses both heavily and means different things by them.

The unit is load-bearing enough that policies bind it directly:
[provenance-lives-in-metadata](/meta/policy/provenance-lives-in-metadata.md)
forbids origin prose "in the `index.md` gloss that lists the doc", and
[negate-only-explicit-cases](/meta/policy/negate-only-explicit-cases.md) scopes
itself to "document bodies, index glosses, and agent responses alike". Because a
directory's [index](/beliefs/glossary/index.md) is what a reader meets before any
document in it, the gloss is often the only sentence consulted — which is why the
[single-overview convention](/beliefs/glossary/single-overview-convention.md)
makes a glossary term's `description` and its index gloss the same text rather
than two drifting summaries.
