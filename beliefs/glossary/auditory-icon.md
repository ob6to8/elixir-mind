# auditory icon

The design principle is *everyday listening*: people hear events and their
causes rather than acoustic properties, so a sound whose physics resemble the
referent's carries meaning without instruction. That immediacy is the whole
advantage over an [earcon](/beliefs/glossary/earcon.md), and it buys a matching
limitation — the technique only reaches events with a plausible physical
analogue, and it composes poorly, since there is no grammar of crumples.

Practical designs mix the two, letting resemblance carry what it can and
abstract motifs carry the rest: a data exfiltration rendered as a sound moving
*outward* and away is icon-like in its spatial logic while remaining an
abstract gesture.
