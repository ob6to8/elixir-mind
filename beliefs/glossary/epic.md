# epic

Standard Agile/Scrum vocabulary for a body of work too large to land in one
pass, decomposed into smaller units (stories, tasks, commits) but tracked and
completed as a whole. James C. Davis's *Model-Based Agentic Software
Engineering* (MAGE) applies the same shape to agent-dispatched work: an epic
is the unit a [definition of done](/beliefs/glossary/definition-of-done.md)
and a [semantic gap](/beliefs/glossary/semantic-gap.md)-aware governance check
attach to, since properties like "the model matches the code" only need to
hold once the whole epic is finished, not at every intermediate commit inside
it.
