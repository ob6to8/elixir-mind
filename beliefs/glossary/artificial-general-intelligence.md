# artificial general intelligence

Usually abbreviated **AGI**; its sibling coinage **ASI** (artificial
superintelligence) names the further step where capability surpasses the best
humans in every domain. Because neither term fixes a measurable criterion —
recognition is deferred to "we'll know it when we see it" — both function in
public discourse as rhetorical and investment devices as much as technical
milestones, absorbing whatever hopes or fears lie beyond the current
capability horizon.
