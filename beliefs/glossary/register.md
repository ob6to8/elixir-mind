# register

1. **A record roll** — the entries are the point: each row is one tracked item
   with enough metadata (status, tags, reasoning) to act on without consulting
   a fuller document.
2. **A linguistic register** — the vocabulary and density a
   context calls for, as in the *technical register* the
   [plainspeak orientation](/beliefs/glossary/plainspeak-orientation.md)
   deliberately delays.

**In this brain:** sense 1 names the maintained rolls — the
[bookmarks register](/survey/bookmarks.md) of the survey tier, the
[channels register](/survey/channels.md), and the
[banned-phrases register](/meta/policy/banned-phrases.md), whose entries carry
their generalized pattern and the reasoning that banned them.
