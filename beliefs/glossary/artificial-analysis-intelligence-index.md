# Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index

Its authority comes from being vendor-independent and uniformly administered:
labs benchmark their own releases under conditions they choose, so a third party
running every model through the same harness is what makes "third overall" a
meaningful sentence at all. This is why it, rather than a model card's own
numbers, is the usual citation when placing a release against the field.

What the single number excludes is as important as what it contains. Cost,
latency, and reliability are all scored separately — Kimi K3 sits third on the
index while costing more per task than models beneath it and
[hallucinating](/beliefs/glossary/hallucination.md) more than its own predecessor
— so treating the index as a proxy for deployability inverts what it measures.
