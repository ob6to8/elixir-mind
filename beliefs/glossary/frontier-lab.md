# frontier lab

The term carries an economic claim as much as a technical one: holding the
frontier requires training runs whose capital cost only a few organizations can
sustain, so the label implies a race dynamic — competition for talent, compute,
and benchmark leadership. That advantage is pressured from below when
[open-weights](/beliefs/glossary/open-weights.md) challengers approach flagship
performance, since inference then stops differentiating by orders of magnitude
and intelligence trends toward a commodity.
