# doxastic logic

Written `B_a φ` — *agent a believes φ* — and distinguished from its neighbours
by exactly what it drops: **alethic** modality concerns necessity and
possibility, **epistemic** modality (`K_a φ`, knowledge) classically requires
that φ actually hold, and doxastic modality requires neither. An agent can
believe what is false; the formula stays well-formed.

That indifference is what makes it the right anchor for a
[belief](/beliefs/glossary/belief-type.md): the warrant is the holder's
commitment, not evidence, which is precisely why such a statement sits outside
the [verification ladder](/beliefs/glossary/verification-ladder.md) rather than
low on it. Where a formal vocabulary for organizing knowledge is silent on
this axis — as ISO 704, SKOS, PROV, and SBVR all are — this is the tradition
that names it.
