# parity principle

Its force is that it moves the question from *where* a process happens to *what
role* it plays, denying location any privileged status in deciding what belongs
to a mind. It is the argumentative engine of the
[extended mind](/beliefs/glossary/extended-mind.md), and the standard objection
runs the other way: critics accept the reasoning and reject its scope, holding
that the resemblance between remembering and looking something up is too coarse
to license the conclusion.
