# einstellung-effect

From the German for "attitude/set"; demonstrated in Abraham Luchins's
water-jar experiments (1942), where subjects trained on a multi-step formula
kept applying it to problems a simpler method solved.

**In this brain:** named as the human-side analog of an agentic failure
observed live in the [TDD research-spike thread](/meta/threads/2026-08-01-tdd-research-spike-and-methodology-adoption.md):
a frame formed earlier in a session (a length-cap framing of PR size) kept
steering later prose after its premise had been explicitly retracted, because
an append-only context has no retraction operation — a superseded premise and
its successor coexist as tokens, and the in-progress line of thought usually
holds more salience. The missing faculty has a classical-AI name —
[truth maintenance](/beliefs/glossary/truth-maintenance-system.md) — and the
working mediations are derive-don't-recall rules and fresh context per
[matter](/beliefs/glossary/matter.md). Slated for the agentic
cognitive-biases compendium (planned; see the thread's routing ledger).
