# bound adaptation

This brain's own [doctrine](/beliefs/glossary/doctrine.md) against silent
policy drift: persistent state and locally-approved fixes accumulate into a
new effective policy unless every material adaptation is bound to a trigger
and revalidated against an immutable behavioral contract. The operating
contract here is **ratification-immutable** — immutable to agents through
every channel, mutable through exactly one, operator ratification — so no
pathway by which an agent's local fix could become standing behavior without
that ratification is permitted to exist unclosed. Canonically defined in the
[bound-adaptation doctrine](/meta/doctrine/bound-adaptation.md).

The doctrine is what rules out score-gated self-modification (an agent
patching itself because a measured metric improved) as a class, regardless of
how well-designed the gate: a measured score is not the operator, and
substituting one for the other is exactly the silent adaptation channel the
doctrine exists to close.
