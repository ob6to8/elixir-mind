# stylometry

Classically used for authorship attribution of disputed texts; its current
mass application is AI-writing detection — reading for "LLM tells"
(characteristic phrasings, em-dash density, hedging patterns) and discounting
text that shows them. As an authorship test it is inference from surface
features, so it is both unverifiable in the individual case and an arms race:
the tells are training artifacts, removable by the next fine-tune or a light
rewrite. The [human-writing-attribution project](/projects/human-writing-attribution.md)
positions disclosed-process attribution as the structural alternative to
stylometric inference.
