# journal namespace

Filed by the [`/journal` skill](/.claude/skills/journal/SKILL.md): one file per day (`journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, `type: note`), same-day additions appended, anchored by date rather than inbound links. A requested agent response is persisted verbatim beneath the entry under a marked `## Response` heading — operator voice above, agent voice below, never interleaved. Third instance of the [non-bundle namespace](/beliefs/glossary/non-bundle-namespace.md) mechanics after the [inbox namespace](/beliefs/glossary/inbox-namespace.md) and the [survey tier](/beliefs/glossary/survey-tier.md); unlike those staging tiers, journal entries are terminal records, not candidates for promotion. Exists to counter [synthesis by proxy](/beliefs/glossary/synthesis-by-proxy.md), leaning on [retrieval practice](/beliefs/glossary/retrieval-practice.md).
