# source recall

Borrowed from information retrieval, where recall is the share of relevant
documents a query returns, and narrowed here to *primary* sources for a research
question whose complete answer set is known.

The measurement sits **upstream of reporting discipline**. A rule asking an
agent to caveat what it did not find is only addressable when recall is high
enough that the agent could know what it missed; when recall is low, the agent
is being asked to caveat around a gap it cannot see, and the remedy belongs in
tooling instead.
