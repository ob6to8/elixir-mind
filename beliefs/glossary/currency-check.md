# currency check

The section lives *inside* the reference doc (an `## <year> currency check`
heading), not as a sibling analysis — the aging verdict travels with the capture
it judges, so a reader always sees the source and its shelf-life together. The
date in the heading and the "researched N months after publication" opener are
load-bearing: the check is itself a dated claim that will age, and a future
session revisiting the doc appends or revises against that stamp. Verdicts are
per-claim, not per-doc — a source is typically part-confirmed, part-qualified,
part-superseded, and the check says which is which. Established by the
[Anthropic context-engineering essay capture](/knowledge/SWE/agentic/agentic-loop/effective-context-engineering-for-agents.md)
(PR #10) and reused by the
[Manus lessons capture](/knowledge/SWE/agentic/context-engineering/context-engineering-lessons-from-manus.md).
