# ISO 704

Its subject matter is the chain from *object* to *concept* to *definition* to
*designation*, and its craft rules — the substitution test, the ban on
circularity, the distinction between defining a concept and merely describing
one — are what a description grammar can be built on. Two properties matter
for how it is used here. It carries **no truth-status axis**: a concept is a
unit of knowledge, and whether propositions about it are established is simply
outside its scope, which is why it constrains the
[concept](/beliefs/glossary/concept-type.md) type cleanly while saying nothing
about the propositional [statement types](/beliefs/glossary/statement-type.md)
or about [beliefs](/beliefs/glossary/belief-type.md). And it is a **paid** standard
(edition 4, 2022; CHF 204), so this bundle holds it the way it holds any
copyrighted source — principles distilled, phrase-scale quotes, the document
itself never committed.
