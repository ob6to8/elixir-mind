# truth-apt

The distinction is about eligibility, not accuracy: "all swans are white" is
truth-apt and false, while "let X denote a swan" is neither. A definition is
assessed as adequate or inadequate, a directive as obeyed or flouted — neither
admits a truth value, so attaching a verification flag to one is a category
error rather than a merely unanswered question.

Applied to a controlled type vocabulary, it draws a hard line: the types whose
content is propositional can carry evidence, and the rest must not be given
the field at all. Among truth-apt statements a second, softer axis remains —
**assertoric strength**, how firmly the author stands behind the proposition —
which is what separates a claim advanced for reliance from a note recording
an observation — the two propositional members of the
[statement types](/beliefs/glossary/statement-type.md) — without either one
changing kind.
