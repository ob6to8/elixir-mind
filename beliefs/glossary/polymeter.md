# polymeter

The session's recurring example of what the ramp model makes parametric: from
one bar-phase, `edge(phase * 3)` against a four-on-the-floor kick yields a
figure that resolves with the bar only every three bars — on sound, on video
cuts (an edit cycling in 3 against music in 4, per
[ramps as time](/knowledge/media-production/sequencing/ramps-as-time.md)), and
speculatively on orchestration, where two agent loops with incommensurate
periods produce the phase-drift signature the
[declared-cadence analysis](/meta/analysis/declared-cadence-swarm-auditability.md)
maps to livelock detection.
