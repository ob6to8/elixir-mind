# plainspeak orientation

Canonically defined by the
[plainspeak-orientation policy](/meta/policy/plainspeak-orientation.md). The
orientation is a whole short account, never phrase-by-phrase interleaving —
phrase-scale unpacking stays on demand via
[`/elaborate`](/.claude/skills/elaborate/SKILL.md) — and it is a derivation of
the technical content at lower resolution, the response-surface sibling of the
plain tier in
[three-level documentation](/beliefs/glossary/three-level-documentation.md)
(whose motivating reader is in
[constant tutorial mode](/beliefs/glossary/constant-tutorial-mode.md)).
