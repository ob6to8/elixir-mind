# coupled system

The term is Clark and Chalmers'; its work in their argument is to relocate the
boundary of a cognitive system away from the organism, since every component
"plays an active causal role" and the components jointly govern behavior. It is
the unit the [extended mind](/beliefs/glossary/extended-mind.md) posits, where
the [parity principle](/beliefs/glossary/parity-principle.md) is the test that
licenses positing it.

**In this brain:** the pairing runs three ways rather than two — operator,
bundle, and the agent that reads and writes the bundle. That third element
distinguishes a [second mind](/beliefs/glossary/second-mind.md) from a second
brain and is why alignment, being a relation across the whole pairing, survives
in none of the parts alone (see
[second mind as extended cognition with an executor](/knowledge/knowledge-management/extended-cognition/second-mind-as-extended-cognition-with-an-executor.md)).
