# LoCoMo

Its cautionary career matters more than its mechanics: Mem0 and Zep each
published accusations that the other misconfigured it, an independent audit
counted a hard ceiling near 93.57% from wrong gold answers, and Letta showed
plain files-plus-grep beating a leading extraction pipeline on it — making
it the field's standing example of vendor-run benchmark numbers as marketing
surface. [LongMemEval](/beliefs/glossary/longmemeval.md) is the
vendor-independent instrument usually preferred against it.
