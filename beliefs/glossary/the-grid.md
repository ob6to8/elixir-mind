# the Grid

Canonically bounded in
[the Grid's programmability boundary](/knowledge/media-production/daw-control/bitwig/the-grid-programmability-boundary.md):
parameter-addressable, not patch-addressable — performed through its exported
surface, with DSP topology changed by preset swap. Distinct from the
*declared timing grid* of
[code-driven AV production](/projects/code-driven-av-production.md), which is
a time lattice, not a modular environment.
