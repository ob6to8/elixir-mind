# model-as-a-service

The category matters chiefly as a licensing boundary. It is the one activity in
which a third party captures revenue directly from someone else's weights while
adding nothing but serving capacity, which is why open-weight licenses
increasingly single it out —
[revenue-tiered licenses](/beliefs/glossary/revenue-tiered-license.md) commonly
gate MaaS above a threshold while exempting purely internal deployment and
application-layer products.

The distinction from internal use is drawn on exposure to third parties, not on
scale or profit: a company running a downloaded model for its own staff is not
operating MaaS however large it is.
