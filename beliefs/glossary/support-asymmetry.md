# support asymmetry

Shape-level checking cannot see this defect: every `verified_by` target
resolves and every quoted span is genuinely verbatim, yet part of what the
reader takes away rests on nothing captured. The worked instance is the
open-weights pricing claim, where five source captures ground one side of
"prices at Claude Sonnet 5 parity" and no capture anywhere states the other
side's prices — the anchor rode in on model memory. Kin cases from the same
audit: a title or `description` quietly widening past what the targets say,
and a list written as if complete when the sources only license "not by
X". The countermeasures are editorial, per the
[epistemology review](/meta/analysis/epistemology-and-governance-review.md):
a comparative claim captures both sides, and a description stays inside its
evidence.
