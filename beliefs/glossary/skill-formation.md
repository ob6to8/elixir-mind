# skill formation

The term carries the study-design framing: skill formation is measured per
task (what did the practitioner learn doing this?), which leaves open whether
AI-driven losses per task are offset by completing more tasks across more
systems — the volume-compensation question. Its pessimistic neighbor is
*deskilling*; its mechanism-level neighbor here is
[cognitive debt](/beliefs/glossary/cognitive-debt.md), and the delegation-side
version of the stake — expertise forms in exactly the detail-work being handed
off — is the hand-off argument under
[expertise](/knowledge/SWE/agentic/expertise/index.md).
