# synthesis by proxy

Named as the failure mode the operator's first journal entry diagnosed: an agent effective at sourcing, surfacing, summarizing, and presenting invites the user into pure consumption, and the danger is precisely that the substitution is easy, not that it is hard. The [journal namespace](/beliefs/glossary/journal-namespace.md) is the counter-practice; the related presentation-side inversion is [agent-as-presenter](/beliefs/glossary/agent-as-presenter.md), and the accumulated cost is [cognitive debt](/beliefs/glossary/cognitive-debt.md).
