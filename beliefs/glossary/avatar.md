# avatar

The word reaches computing from Sanskrit by way of 1980s virtual worlds, where
what mattered was that other participants encountered the figure rather than the
human driving it — presence by delegation.

**In this brain:** the operator's framing for what a personal knowledge system
becomes once it is published and consulted by others. On that reading the tree's
shape, the policies, and the ratification history amount to a portrait of what
its operator attends to and how they decide, so the artifact carries their
judgment into a space they are not personally present in. The framing is what
makes reproducibility unthreatening — a fork copies the structure but not the
expectations it was tuned against — and what raises the stakes on anything the
system publishes in the operator's name.
