# earcon

The word is a pun on *icon*. Because the mapping is arbitrary, earcons compose:
motifs can be transposed, layered, or sequenced to build a small grammar in
which a family relationship between two events is heard as a family
relationship between two sounds. That compositional reach is what a naturalistic
[auditory icon](/beliefs/glossary/auditory-icon.md) trades away for immediate
recognizability.

The cost is a training requirement — an earcon vocabulary is undecodable until
its legend is published, which is why a
[sonification](/beliefs/glossary/sonification.md) built on earcons owes its
audience an audible key. They are the right encoding for events too *rare* to
sustain a continuous stream: a distinctive gesture heard six times across
seventeen thousand actions is unmissable, where the same six events occupy an
invisible fraction of a bar chart.
