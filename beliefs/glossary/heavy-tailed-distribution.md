# heavy-tailed distribution

In talent contexts the phrase does specific work: if individual output is
heavy-tailed, a small number of top performers contribute most of the total,
which justifies concentrating pay and hiring effort on them — the premise
behind the claim that
[raising the floor of the weakest engineers](/knowledge/SWE/agentic/expertise/ai-makes-weak-engineers-less-harmful.md)
changes team economics without touching the top of the distribution.
