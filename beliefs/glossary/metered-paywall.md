# metered paywall

The "metering" is the per-visitor counter, contrasted with a *hard* paywall (no
free reads at all — e.g. The Information) and with *freemium* publishing (the
author chooses per-piece which posts are free — e.g. Substack newsletters).
Medium is the canonical metered case, compounded by per-piece member-only
flags: an unpaid reader gets a few stories a month, and stories the author
marks member-only need a paid Medium membership regardless of quota. For an
agent scanning sources, the practical consequence is that a metered or paid
channel can only be surveyed from its publicly readable surface — titles,
teasers, public posts — never summarized as if the gated body had been read.
