# granular synthesis

Its usefulness for [sonification](/beliefs/glossary/sonification.md) is that one
control — grain rate — spans two perceptual regimes continuously. At low rates
the grains are heard individually as countable events; past the fusion threshold
they merge and the same parameter is heard as roughness and brightness instead
(see
[auditory stream segregation](/beliefs/glossary/auditory-stream-segregation.md)).
A quantity that ranges over orders of magnitude therefore needs no rescaling and
no binning: the display switches from counting to texture on its own, and the
crossover itself becomes meaningful when the data's story is a transition from
discrete acts to continuous flow.
