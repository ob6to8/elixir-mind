# Artificial Analysis — Kimi K3 achieves #3 in the Intelligence Index

On the index placement:

> Kimi K3 scores 57 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Its
> intelligence is comparable to Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 but remains behind Fable 5
> and GPT-5.6 Sol.

On the open-weights field, naming the leading alternatives by index score:

> GLM-5.2 (51) and DeepSeek v4 Pro (44)

On cost and token efficiency across the index run:

> Cost per task ($0.94) is similar to GPT-5.6 Sol ($1.04), ~1/2 the price of
> Opus 4.8 ($1.80).

> Kimi K3's token usage on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index decreased
> significantly, using 21% fewer output tokens than K2.6.

The model page for the same evaluation adds Artificial Analysis's own summary
judgement and the run's cost:

> Kimi K3 is amongst the leading models in intelligence, but particularly

> notably slow and very verbose.

> In total, it cost $2437.41 to evaluate Kimi K3 on the Intelligence Index.
