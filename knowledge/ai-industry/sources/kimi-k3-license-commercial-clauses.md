# Kimi K3 License — the commercial threshold clauses

The revenue gate, quoting the threshold clause:

> the aggregate revenue of the Licensee and its affiliates exceeds 20 million US
> dollars (or the equivalent in other currencies)

Crossing that threshold — measured over any consecutive twelve months on a
Model-as-a-Service business — requires entering a separate agreement with
Moonshot AI before any commercial use of the software or its derivative works.

The branding requirement obliges commercial products or services exceeding
either 100 million monthly active users or 20 million US dollars in monthly
revenue to prominently display **"Kimi K3"** on the user interface.

The exemptions carve out purely internal use never exposed to third parties, and
access obtained through Moonshot AI's own official products or its certified
inference partners.
