# ISO 704:2022 — Terminology work: Principles and methods

ISO 704 (4th edition, July 2022) establishes the basic principles and methods
for preparing and compiling terminologies both inside and outside the
framework of standardization. It covers the relationships between an object,
the concept formed of it, its definition, and its designation (term/name) —
and lays down rules for how terms and proper names should be formed and how
definitions should be written.

It applies broadly: terminology work in scientific, technological,
industrial, legal, administrative, and other fields of knowledge — explicitly
not the presentation format for terminological entries inside other
International Standards, which is instead governed by ISO 10241-1/-2.

## Why this is relevant here

This is precisely the discipline this bundle's own controlled `type`
vocabulary and glossary practice are informally reinventing — a fixed
relationship between a concept, its definition, and the term used to
designate it, with change governed by ratification rather than ad hoc
renaming (see [prefer established terminology](/meta/policy/prefer-established-terminology.md)
and the [glossary](/beliefs/glossary.md)).
