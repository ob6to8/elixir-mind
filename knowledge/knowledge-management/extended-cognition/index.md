# extended-cognition

The philosophy of mind that personal knowledge systems inherit from: when an
external artifact counts as part of a cognitive system, what conditions the
coupling must meet, and what changes when the artifact gains an executor.

## Claims

- [Second mind as extended cognition with an executor](/knowledge/knowledge-management/extended-cognition/second-mind-as-extended-cognition-with-an-executor.md) — the second-brain/second-mind split restates the extended-mind criteria; the new element is an executor acting on the store under checks that make automatic endorsement safe.

## Sources

- [Clark & Chalmers (1998), "The Extended Mind" — key passages](/knowledge/knowledge-management/extended-cognition/clark-chalmers-1998-the-extended-mind.md) — verbatim: active externalism, the parity principle, the coupled-system claim, Otto's notebook, and the four criteria for extended belief.
