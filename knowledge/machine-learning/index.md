# machine-learning

Technical machine-learning knowledge — models, architectures, training methods, and
the theory behind them. (Distinct from [ai-industry](/knowledge/ai-industry/index.md), which
covers the market and economics of AI, and from
[SWE/llm-engineering](/knowledge/SWE/llm-engineering/index.md), which covers making LLM
inference and retrieval cheaper, faster, or more capable in practice.)

## Contents

- [agent-based-modeling](/knowledge/machine-learning/agent-based-modeling/index.md) —
  emergent coordination from local or pairwise agent rules: flocking, swarming,
  and related complex-systems simulation
- [ai-and-mathematics](/knowledge/machine-learning/ai-and-mathematics/index.md) —
  AI's relationship to mathematics: capability, mechanism, and the institutional
  effects on the profession
- [deep-learning](/knowledge/machine-learning/deep-learning/index.md) — neural-network
  architectures and the methods used to train them
- [evolutionary-computation](/knowledge/machine-learning/evolutionary-computation/index.md) —
  genetic algorithms, evolution strategies, and other population/distribution-based
  search methods used inside or alongside ML systems
