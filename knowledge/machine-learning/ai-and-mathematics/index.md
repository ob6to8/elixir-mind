# AI and mathematics

References on AI's relationship to mathematics — capability, mechanism, and
the institutional effects on the profession.

## Contents

- [AI isn't outthinking mathematicians, it's out-remembering them (Davide Piffer)](/knowledge/machine-learning/ai-and-mathematics/ai-out-remembers-not-outthinks-mathematicians.md) — AI's math strength as working-memory capacity (context-window-as-notebook) rather than superior reasoning.
- [The End of Mathematics — AI and the collapse of research incentives (Daniel Litt)](/knowledge/machine-learning/ai-and-mathematics/end-of-mathematics-institutional-incentives.md) — a cautionary scenario in which AI-accelerated output outpaces incentives built for scarcity, hollowing out the profession from within.
- [Mathematics in the age of AI (Terence Tao)](/knowledge/machine-learning/ai-and-mathematics/mathematics-in-the-age-of-ai-tao.md) — a five-stage view of mathematical work (generation, verification, exposition, publication, canonicalization) and a standard that a proof is incomplete unless a human expert can explain it. _(source)_
