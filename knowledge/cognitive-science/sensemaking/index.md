# Sensemaking

How people build usable understanding of a fast-changing, uncertain situation
without waiting for — or needing — accurate prediction: regulating attention,
building small deliberately-partial models, and acting on field reports
rather than forecasts.

## Contents

- [How to Make Sense of AI (Cedric Chin / Commoncog)](/knowledge/cognitive-science/sensemaking/how-to-make-sense-of-ai.md) — ignore opinions and forecasts, attend only to detailed field reports of use, and interrogate each with four fixed questions about outcomes, actions, relative value, and causal mechanism.
