# biases

Documented human cognitive biases — the empirical finding, its classic
demonstration, and its mechanism, each cross-linked to any agent-side analog
filed under [failure-modes](/knowledge/SWE/agentic/failure-modes/index.md).

## Contents

- [Einstellung effect](/knowledge/cognitive-science/biases/einstellung-effect.md) —
  a practiced or primed solution approach keeps being applied after the
  conditions that justified it changed; the first activated schema keeps
  directing attention toward itself (Luchins' water jars; Bilalić et al.'s
  eye-tracking). `em:48e815` _(concept)_
- [Continued influence effect](/knowledge/cognitive-science/biases/continued-influence-effect.md) —
  information encoded as true and later retracted keeps influencing
  inference even when the retraction is recalled; a retraction is an
  addition that must out-compete the original trace, and systematically
  fails to. `em:3c861f` _(concept)_
