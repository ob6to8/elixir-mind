# cognitive-science

How human cognition works and fails — empirical findings and explanatory
frameworks from cognitive psychology, filed as first-class knowledge and
serving as the human-side anchors for the agent-failure mappings under
[failure-modes](/knowledge/SWE/agentic/failure-modes/index.md).

## Contents

- [biases](/knowledge/cognitive-science/biases/index.md) — documented human
  cognitive biases: their classic demonstrations and mechanisms, each
  cross-linked to any agent-side analog
- [Resource-rational analysis](/knowledge/cognitive-science/resource-rational-analysis.md) —
  Lieder & Griffiths' framework reading many biases as optimal trade-offs
  under bounded computation — the shared pressure that makes bias-like
  shortcuts expectable in any resource-limited reasoner. `em:24b4fd` _(concept)_
