# The Rust Programming Language — Brown University edition

An experimental fork of
[the official Rust book](/knowledge/SWE/rust/rust-lang-org-learn.md),
maintained by Brown University's Cognitive Engineering Lab and periodically
resynced against the upstream text, that adds interactive-learning
instrumentation on top of the same content.

## What it adds over the original

- **Inline quizzes** at concept boundaries, used by the lab to gather data on
  where learners actually get confused.
- **Text annotation** — readers can highlight passages and leave comments.
- **A rewritten "Understanding Ownership" chapter**, using diagrams generated
  by the lab's own **Aquascope** tool to visualize ownership/borrowing state
  instead of relying on prose alone.

## Research backing

The rewrite is not just presentational — edits are targeted at
misconceptions the lab identified from quiz-response data, and the project
has produced peer-reviewed output: a distinguished-paper award at OOPSLA 2024
("Profiling Programming Language Learning") and a 2023 research highlight on
conceptual models of Rust's ownership types.
