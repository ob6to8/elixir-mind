# It's not empowering to hand off the details

David Nicholas Williams's short essay is a rebuttal to a premise he sees
underneath much of the enthusiasm for AI: the dream of manifesting good
outcomes "without having to get into the details."

## The argument

**Abstraction does not remove complexity, it only hides it.** "The closer you
look at anything, the messier and more nuanced it gets" — so no level of
delegation, LLM-based or otherwise, actually escapes the detail, it only
defers when you meet it.

**Knowing what's safe to hand off requires already being good at the thing.**
You can hand off *some* of the details, but discerning which "some" is itself
the skill: "to be good at something is to know, or be able to work out, which
'some'." Someone not yet good at a task cannot make that judgment, which means
effective delegation presupposes the expertise it is being used to avoid
acquiring.

**This creates a closed loop, not a shortcut.** Becoming good at something
"requires a complete reversal of the mindset that would lead one to having
wanted to hand it off" in the first place — obsessive attention to detail is,
in his framing, the only mechanism by which expertise forms. LLMs join a
lineage of technologies that promised to sever skill from attention to detail,
and he expects the same failure: "reality doesn't work that way, and there's
no reason it should."

**The empowerment framing is inverted.** Success at fully handing off the
details is, by his account, indistinguishable from having "played no role;
have done nothing at all" — the opposite of empowerment, not an instance of
it.

## Relation to the brain's existing material

The essay argues the same shape of claim the brain already holds from the
other side: [comprehension of generated code](/meta/doctrine/comprehension-of-generated-code.md)
holds that comprehension is no longer a free side effect of production once
generation is delegated, and must be deliberately produced — Williams's claim
is the stronger, prior one, that the *capacity to delegate well* is itself
gated by expertise that only forms through the pre-delegation grind. It reads
as a caution against skipping straight to
[step 2+ of the AI-adoption ladder](/knowledge/SWE/agentic/adoption/steps-of-ai-adoption.md)
without having done the step-1 work that builds the judgment later steps
depend on.
